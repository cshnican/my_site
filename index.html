Sihan Chen

Center for Brain and Cognition · Universitat Pompeu Fabra

Hi!

I am a research support technician (postdoc) working with Damián Blasi. I study how extra-linguistic forces, such as policy, social structure, and the pressures of communication, shape the structural diversity of language.

I completed my PhD in August 2026 in the Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences at MIT, with Ted Gibson in TedLab. Before that I studied mechanical engineering at the University of Miami and began language research with Caleb Everett.

Email Google Scholar GitHub LinkedIn Twitter

Outside work I also do photography with a Canon EOS T8i (night sky, hiking, cities) and translations of song lyrics.
